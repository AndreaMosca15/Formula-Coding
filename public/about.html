When Racing and Coding are Life

Since I was little, I've lived with a computer in my hands, thanks to my father's passion for computer science. I used to look at my father's computer with the eyes of a child who doesn't understand what he's looking at, and it was precisely that desire to understand it that made me fall in love — first with hardware, then with software.

Another passion I've carried with me since I was a kid is cars, which later grew into a passion for the world of motorsport git puas well that brought me to start Sim Racing very seriously.

I love solving problems and bringing to life the logic that makes what I design actually work.

I really enjoy talking with people — often older than me — who have interesting experiences and projects to share, so we can exchange ideas and advice and keep growing our personal know-how. I believe that in the era we live in, being able to communicate with people is becoming more and more essential in order to connect with them, especially in a professional setting.

Just think of the tool that's steadily gaining importance in every field: AI. To make AI work, you need to talk to it, and to give it the chance to fully create what we asked for, we need one particular skill: the ability to communicate clearly, to make ourselves understood.

If I had to choose right now what I'd like to do for work, I'd probably go with backend developer (or a race car driver, but that would take a miracle!)
